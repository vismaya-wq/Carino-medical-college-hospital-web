Section 01

Organisation

About Resurgent India Trust

The trust sponsoring this proposal brings together planning for medical education infrastructure and associated clinical facilities. Its role is to steward institutional development, academic partnerships, and transparent communication with stakeholders.

Why establish this institution? To address the need for structured medical education within the region, paired with supervised clinical exposure through a teaching hospital model that can grow responsibly over time.

Purpose

Project purpose and institutional vision

Vision

To cultivate competent, ethical physicians through rigorous education and clinically grounded training—serving public health with long-term institutional commitment.

Mission

To plan and develop an integrated medical college and teaching hospital with disciplined governance, faculty-led academics, and patient-respectful clinical learning environments.

Clinical link

Education and healthcare are intentionally linked: the hospital is not a separate commercial entity, but the clinical complement to the college's academic mandate.
